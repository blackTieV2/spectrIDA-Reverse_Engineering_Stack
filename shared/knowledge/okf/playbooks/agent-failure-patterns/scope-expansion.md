# scope-expansion

**What happens:** A narrow task quietly became a broad refactor.

**Required behaviour:** Stay bounded to the approved scope. If the task 'needs' more, propose it — don't do it.

**If you notice it happening:** stop, record an episodic record
(`shared/templates/episodic-record.template.md`), and surface it to the user.
